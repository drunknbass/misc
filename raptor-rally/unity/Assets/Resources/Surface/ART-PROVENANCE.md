# Coyote Basin ground material

`CoyoteDirt.png` is original generated artwork created September 28, 2026 with the built-in imagegen tool. It is consumed as a 1024px mipmapped albedo with anisotropic filtering, blended at two world-space scales in `RallyDirt.shader`. Micro-normal variation and racing-line wear are separate shader inputs. The source image is preserved unedited; Unity handles platform texture import.

Prompt: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless tileable albedo texture for a Unity off-road racing game terrain, square 1024x1024. Primary request: a detailed original material scan of compacted warm brown stadium dirt with tiny irregular pale stones, granular sandy grit, subtle darker compacted patches and gentle scuffed variations. Perfectly overhead orthographic flat texture covering the full frame uniformly edge to edge. Flat diffuse neutral lighting, no directional light, no shadows, no ambient occlusion baked in, no vignette. Natural subdued ochre-brown, not orange or yellow. Realistic fine-scale aggregate and subtle irregular wear, not a pattern of dots. Seamlessly tileable on all four edges. No large rocks, no vegetation, no objects, no text, no logos, no perspective, no prominent tire tread or long grooves; the game adds racing-line wear separately.
